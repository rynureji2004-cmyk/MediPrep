Hospital Procedure Readiness Platform

Prepare Patients.
Improve Procedure Readiness.

MediPrep is a web-based hospital procedure preparation system that helps doctors schedule procedures, assign preparation checklists, review patient reports, monitor pre-operative vitals, and track overall readiness.

Everything You Need for Procedure Readiness

Explore the integrated toolsets tailored for hospital administrators, physicians, and scheduled patients.

Procedure Scheduling

Doctors can schedule procedures for patients by specifying the procedure type, date, time, and location.

Procedure-Specific Checklists

The system automatically assigns preparation tasks based on the selected procedure, such as fasting, medication instructions, consent forms, and required reports.

Clinical Reports

Patients can upload required medical documents, which doctors can review and verify through the system.

Readiness Monitoring

The system calculates checklist completion and displays the patient's readiness status as Ready, Warning, Review, or Not Ready.

How the System Works

A unified workflow to coordinate pre-operative preparation across departments.

1
Schedule Procedure

The doctor assigns a clinical procedure to the patient with the required date, time, and location.

2
Assign Checklist

The system automatically creates preparation tasks based on the selected procedure.

3
Patient Prepares

Patients complete preparation tasks, upload required reports, and enter pre-operative vitals.

4
Review Readiness

Doctors review the completed tasks, verify uploaded reports, and monitor the patient's readiness status.

Know When a Patient Is Ready

The system evaluates checklist completion, uploaded report status, and active safety alerts to determine the patient's current readiness status.

  • Automatic Risk Alerts for out-of-range patient vitals
  • Procedure-Specific Checklists based on the selected procedure
  • Doctor Verification of uploaded patient reports
